PebbleKey for Volvo puts your car key on your wrist.

Hold a button on your Pebble:
- UP: unlock
- SELECT (middle): start or stop the climate, to pre-heat or cool the cabin
- DOWN: lock

The screen shows whether the car is locked, its battery level, and whether a door, the boot or the hood is open. While the car charges, it also shows the charge target and the time left.

Made to be hard to trigger by accident:
- Every action needs a hold of about a second, so a bump cannot unlock your car.
- If your phone is more than 200 m from the car, unlocking asks for a second hold first. You can change that distance or switch the check off in the settings.

Setup takes about five minutes. Open PebbleKey on the watch, then open its settings in the Pebble app, tap "Sign in with Volvo" and sign in with your Volvo ID on Volvo's own page. Your password goes only to Volvo. Volvo ends every sign-in after 3 months; then you sign in again.

You need:
- A Volvo with remote services, the kind you can lock from the Volvo Cars app. PebbleKey was developed and tested on an EX30; other models are untested.
- Your phone nearby with an internet connection. The watch reaches the car through your phone and Volvo's servers, not over Bluetooth to the car.

Works on every Pebble, from the original Pebble to Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.

PebbleKey for Volvo is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Volvo Cars. It can unlock your car, so use it at your own risk.
